Sources and notes

Ground: USGS 3DEP lidar. Seam: PA DEP coal seam elevations (Cumberland Mine maps). Panels: PA DEP longwall mining panels. Streams traced from lidar. Trees: PEMA 2022 orthophoto; heights estimated. Buildings: Oak Forest traced from the 2022 orthophoto; elsewhere Microsoft building footprints. Roads, power lines: orthophoto, OpenStreetMap. No vertical exaggeration; the panel is drawn 10× its 7 ft height (seam height estimated); tree and building heights are estimates. Keys: arrows turn the view, + and − zoom, R resets.
